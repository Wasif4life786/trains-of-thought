# data/raw/rail (A2)

Raw inputs for the rail model. Nothing here is hand-edited after the fact: cleaning happens in `packages/rail`.

| File | What | Where from |
|---|---|---|
| `stations.json` | 2,609 UK stations as downloaded | davwheat/uk-railway-stations |
| `routes.json` | 8 corridors, both ways: operator, departure minutes, durations, calling points | modelled (`seed:`), not a published timetable |
| `fares.csv` | walk-up fares and Advance range per route, deliberately messy (pounds as text, mixed labels, duplicates) | rounded estimates |
| `performance.csv` | 23k-row punctuality history, deliberately messy | SYNTHETIC: `npm run generate:performance -w @tot/rail` |
| `snapshot/` | frozen data for the offline demo (M3) | |

## Fare calibration (A2.7, checked 8 Nov 2026)

Published anchors used for the **Advance** ranges (the only fares with a reliable free public source):
- London Euston → Manchester Piccadilly: "from £14" single in advance (thetrainline.com/train-times/london-euston-to-manchester-piccadilly); Trainline results for Sat 21 Nov, seen 7 Nov (14 days out): £52.50–£68 for the 09:13–10:33 trains.
- Edinburgh Waverley → Manchester Piccadilly: "from £18.60" in advance (Trainline); Fri 20 Nov 06:12 seen at £39.20 (13 days out).

Walk-up fares (Anytime, Off-Peak, Super Off-Peak) are **rounded estimates**: no free official fares file or reliable public page was found, and the one aggregator that listed an Off-Peak single (£76.30 for London–Manchester) could not be corroborated. Replace them from the National Rail fares feed when available.

## Quirks we found

- davwheat stations: the field is `long`, not `lon`; casing like "Stoke-On-Trent"; "London Kings Cross" has no apostrophe.
- The timetable (`routes.json`), fares (`fares.csv`) and punctuality history (`performance.csv`) are MODELLED seeds; the history is generated by `npm run generate:performance -w @tot/rail` and cleaned like a real messy export.
- A single route × hour cell has only ~20 observations, so estimates are shrunk toward the weekday class and route averages; test patterns across routes, not on one cell.
- Edinburgh ⇄ Manchester (TransPennine, 194 min) is the second scenario; its Advance range (£18–£28 on sale) was calibrated to Trainline's "from £18.60" and the £39.20 seen 13 days out (see data/raw/rail/README.md, A2.7).
- Realtime Trains (A2.9, `src/rtt.ts`, `src/fetch-rtt.ts`): new API at https://data.rtt.io since 1 Oct 2026, Bearer auth. Our `RTT_TOKEN` is a REFRESH token: trade it at `/api/get_access_token` (short-lived access token). Free tier: `gb-nr` only, 10/min, 100/hour, 1,000/day, and history counts today, so 13 full past days (the 14th is HTTP 400). One request covers a whole London day (max window 23h59).
- Query at the ARRIVAL station with `filterFrom=<origin>`: Delay Repay is judged on `arrival.realtimeAdvertisedLateness` at the destination. Times are London wall clock without an offset.
- Real vs synthetic (13 days to 7 Nov 2026): EUS→MAN only 60% within 15 min, 4.5% cancelled, median 10 min late; the synthetic history said ~88%. Real rows win wherever they exist; synthetic is the prior where real data is thin.
